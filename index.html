How it works

1 · Claim

One sentence that can be true or false, with its author and date.

2 · Data and command

Public data, code at a fixed version, and one command that recomputes the result.

3 · Receipts

Each person who runs the check publishes a receipt: what they ran, where, and what came out.

4 · Status

Derived from the receipts, never set by hand. Nothing is deleted: corrections are added.

Three possible results of a check

REPRODUCED NOT REPRODUCED INSUFFICIENT DATA

A failed check is a result, not a nuisance: it is published like any other. "Insufficient data" means the check could not be completed, which is a different fact from a claim that was checked and failed.

Status of a claim

StatusWhen
AUTHOR ONLYOnly the author has reproduced it.
INDEPENDENTLY REPRODUCED (n)n receipts from people not connected to the author, and no unresolved failure.
DISPUTEDAt least one NOT REPRODUCED receipt that the author has not answered with a published correction.

A receipt (format draft 0)

{
  "claim": "A-001",
  "checker": "name or pseudonym, and any relation to the author",
  "date": "2026-10-13",
  "environment": "macOS 27, Python 3.12.x",
  "command": "make eval",
  "code_version": "commit or tag that was run",
  "result": "REPRODUCED | NOT REPRODUCED | INSUFFICIENT DATA",
  "output_sha256": "hash of the full output",
  "notes": "anything that went wrong or differed"
}

Receipts are kept in the public repository agoranet-work/claims (format: receipt.schema.json). Its history cannot be rewritten, so a receipt cannot disappear unnoticed. Signed receipts (Ed25519) are planned.

Corrections

Errors are corrected by adding to the record, never by erasing it.

13 Oct 2026 — Gravit Verifier. An external reviewer showed, with a reproduction, that verifier v0.5.0 ignored the signed verdict of an evidence record: a record signed "check failed" or "no verdict" counted towards a Verified Fact like "check passed", and in a payment scenario led to automatic execution (test keys; no payment was made). The 40 published test vectors did not cover this. Fixed in v0.6.0 with 7 new vectors; details. The first finding on this square by someone other than the author.
13 Oct 2026 — this website. From June to October 2026 this address described "The Agora Network" as a validation testbed for a protocol called "ERC-8004-GT", with conformance classes, four GitHub repositories and a reference implementation by the Gravit Open Network.
  • ERC-8004 is an Ethereum standard by other authors. "ERC-8004-GT" was not part of it, and the name is no longer used.
  • None of the four linked repositories existed; every link returned "not found".
  • The roadmap (community feedback in Q3 2026, EIP submission) did not take place.
  • A site meant to be neutral carried the imagery of one organisation.
The earlier page remains readable, unchanged: archive/2026-07-05.

Funding PLANNED · NOT YET OPERATING

Checking takes work, and work that is needed should be paid. The model Agora intends to use:

  • Authors pay to be checked. A project that wants its public claims checked pays a fixed, published fee per claim.
  • Checkers are paid per receipt, the same amount whatever the result.
  • A bounty for a confirmed error: a NOT REPRODUCED receipt that leads to a published correction.
  • Payment never buys a result. Fees are fixed in advance and do not depend on the outcome.
